Selected work

Travel Tech · Via Indonesia

Fixing what travelers see — and how fast they see it

Search was slow, and the system promised luggage that wasn't in the ticket. I fixed the top two issues and upgraded the GDS integrations behind them.

GDS integration Search performance Data accuracy

The challenge

When I joined, the platform's number one and number two issues were clear: flight search took far too long, and ticket benefits displayed incorrectly. A fare without checked luggage could show "23 kg included" because benefits had been hardcoded into the system years earlier instead of being read from the airlines and GDS.

What I did

I tackled both from the integration layer. We upgraded the connections to the GDS systems (Sabre, Galileo Travelport) and reworked benefit display to come from live API returns instead of hardcoded values. Alongside the search-time and accuracy fixes, I owned the annual roadmap, wrote PRDs and BRDs, coordinated design, engineering, legal and compliance, and tested features before every release.

What came out of it

Faster search, accurate fare benefits straight from the source, and booking integrations the business could rely on. After the fix went live, complaints about incorrect benefits dropped by around 60%.

Fintech · Komunal

Automating the loan pipeline

Credit scoring was solid — but manual. I turned it into an automated workflow and opened it up to outside lenders via API.

Workflow automation Open API B2B2C

The challenge

Komunal's credit scoring was already good — the bottleneck was that the process around it ran manually. Loan requests from SME owners had to be picked up, tracked and moved through analysis by hand.

What I did

I designed the automated flow: the moment an SME owner submits a loan request, it lands in the credit analysts' system as a to-do, and every progress change updates automatically. I also designed a document-flow mechanism and worked on an open API so external lenders could run their own loan products through the platform.

What came out of it

Shorter waiting times for borrowers, a tidier document trail for analysts, and a platform other lenders could build on.

Agritech · Koltiva

Traceability from farm gate to global brand

Palm oil, seaweed, rubber, coconut, cocoa — I made sure the apps worked where it mattered most: in the field.

B2B SaaS Field ops Digital payments

The challenge

Koltiva's platforms trace commodities — palm oil, seaweed, rubber, coconut, cocoa — from smallholder farms into global supply chains. My job was making sure the software worked well in the field.

What I did

On our GSMA project, we digitalised payments for farmers and commodity traders in rural Indonesia. Separately, I worked on the Nestlé Global application — a track-and-trace platform for sources of dairy, coffee and cocoa. I drove the initiation phase through to the working prototype and winning the tender, before handing it over for delivery.

What came out of it

The farmer base tripled during the project, and 80% of farmers went on to transact digitally. The Nestlé project also resulted in a successful global tender.

Automotive · Mercedes-Benz Indonesia

Solving the case of the missing parts

Assemblies were stalling halfway because parts never arrived. I traced the problem back to the warehouse — and redesigned it.

Lean Layout planning Logistics

The challenge

Assemblies kept stalling mid-build. An operator could be 45% or even 80% through an assembly before discovering a required part was missing.

What I did

I traced the problem upstream. The investigation showed the missing parts had never been sent from the warehouse. I analysed storage and picking flows and redesigned the layout to improve capacity and accessibility. The work became my bachelor's thesis.

What came out of it

A warehouse organised around how parts actually flow to the production line — and an early lesson in root-cause thinking.
